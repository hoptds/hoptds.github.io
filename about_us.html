THE GOAL

HOP (Hatid Order Padala) was founded with one goal in mind: to make everyday transport and delivery simpler, faster, and more reliable for everyone. In busy cities where traffic, heavy rain, peak-hour chaos, and sudden cancellations are part of daily life, people deserve a service they can count on - anytime, anywhere.

What makes HOP different is our heart for local communities. We aim not only to serve customers but also to uplift local transport groups like tricycles and habal-habal drivers by giving them a platform to earn more and be part of the growing on-demand service industry - without creating competition that pushes them out. Instead, we work with them, not against them.

INNOVATORS

HOP began as a vision by Ederlindo Cojuangco, who saw the need for a more reliable transport and delivery solution in the city. What started as a local idea quickly grew into a real project when he met a group of talented graduating students and interns looking for hands-on experience in the tech industry. These students - future designers, developers, and IT professionals - helped turn the concept into a functioning platform. The entire team behind HOP consists of skilled individuals who, despite having no prior workplace experience at the time, pushed themselves to learn, adapt and create something meaningful.

MISSION

HOP exists to make moving around the city effortless - from people to packages - while giving local drivers a fair, dependable way to earn. We're here to cut through the daily chaos of traffic, rain, and long waits by delivering a service that's quick, safe, and rooted in community.

VISION

We envision cities where transport is no longer a struggle but a smooth, accessible experience for everyone. A future where local drivers thrive alongside modern technology, and where HOP becomes the bridge that connects communities, supports livelihoods, and keeps the city moving forward.
