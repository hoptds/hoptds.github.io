Code of Conduct

The HOP Code of Conduct sets clear expectations for riders and driver-partners to ensure a safe, respectful, and reliable transportation experience for everyone.

1. Respect and Professionalism

  • Treat each other with courtesy and respect at all times.
  • Do not engage in harassment, discrimination, or rude behavior.
  • Communicate politely and cooperate during the trip.

2. Safety is a Priority

  • Follow all traffic laws and safety regulations.
  • Drivers must operate vehicles responsibly and avoid reckless or distracted driving.
  • The use of alcohol, drugs, or any unsafe behavior during trips is strictly prohibited.

3. Driver Responsibilities

  • Maintain a valid license and proper vehicle documents.
  • Keep the vehicle clean, safe, and in good condition.
  • Do not ask for extra payments outside the app.
  • Avoid cancelling trips without a valid reason.

4. Rider Responsibilities

  • Provide correct pickup and drop-off details.
  • Be ready on time and treat the driver and vehicle with respect.
  • Do not request drivers to break traffic rules.
  • Pay fares using the official HOP system only.

5. Fair and Honest Use of the Platform

  • Use only your registered account—no fake identities.
  • Do not manipulate bookings, fares, or ratings.
  • All trips must be arranged through the HOP app.

6. Prohibited Actions

The following are not allowed:

  • Violence, threats, or unsafe behavior.
  • Carrying illegal or dangerous items.
  • Damaging vehicles or property.
  • Any activity that harms the safety or trust of the community.

7. Violations

Failure to follow this Code of Conduct may result in:

  • Warnings or suspension of account access.
  • Permanent removal from the platform.
  • Possible legal action if laws are violated.

The HOP Code of Conduct sets clear expectations for riders and driver-partners to ensure a safe, respectful, and reliable transportation experience for everyone.
