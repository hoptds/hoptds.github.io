Getting Started

Riders must create an account to book trips, track rides, and use HOP services.

1.1 How Rider Registration Works

Step 1: Enter Your Mobile Number

Open the HOP App.

On the welcome screen, enter your mobile number and tap Continue.

System Check:

  • New Number: You will be directed to Create Account.
  • Existing Number: You will be redirected to the Log In page.

Step 2: Verify Your Mobile Number (New Users Only)

  • Tap Send OTP.
  • Enter the One-Time Password (OTP) received via SMS.

This confirms that the number belongs to you.

Step 3: Enter a Referral Code (Optional)

After OTP verification, you may see a screen asking: "Wanna use a referral code?"

  • Enter the referral code if you have one
  • If the code is invalid, an error message will appear
  • Tap Skip to continue without entering a code

This step is optional and only appears once.

Step 4: Provide Personal Information

  • Full Name (as shown on your ID)
  • Email Address (optional but recommended)
  • Profile Photo (optional)

Step 5: Face Verification

You will be guided to a screen labeled "Verify with Selfie", matching the UI shown in the app.

This step ensures account security and prevents fraudulent activity.

You will be asked to:

  • Center your face within the frame
  • Follow simple instructions (e.g., blink, turn slightly)
  • Wait for a verification loading screen to complete

Once successful, you will see a confirmation message.

Step 6: Account Confirmation

You will see a "Welcome to HOP" screen.

Your account is now ready for booking rides.

Apply through the Driver onboarding flow, submit all required documents, and wait for account verification and approval.

Go to Account & Security, choose Deactivate Account, and follow the prompts to confirm your request.

Selfie verification protects your account and helps confirm that only you can access HOP services.

Logging in to your HOP account is quick and secure using your mobile number and a One-Time Password (OTP).

How to Log In:

  1. Open the HOP App and ensure you're connected to the internet.
  2. Enter your registered mobile number on the login screen.
  3. Tap "Send OTP." A 6-digit code will be sent to your SMS inbox.
  4. Check your messages and enter the OTP exactly as received.
  5. The system will automatically verify the code and log you in.
  6. Once verified, you will be redirected to your HOP dashboard.

Open Profile Settings from the app menu and update the fields you want to change before saving.

  1. To deactivate your account simply go to the app's menu tab and click Account & Security.
  2. Then choose Deactivate Account.
  3. After that, a list of options will be presented to you. You have to choose the reason why you wanted to deactivate your account.
  4. You can also choose Others and specify the reason on the input box provided if your reason for deactivation is not in the choices.
  5. Next, once you have chosen a reason for deactivation just hit the red button below and type DEACTIVATE.
  6. Lastly, it will prompt you that your account will be deactivated after 60 days and will log you out automatically.
