Transport

Open the app → choose ride type → set pickup and destination → review fare → tap Confirm Booking → wait for a driver to accept.

Tap Cancel Ride on the booking screen → select a reason → confirm cancellation. Fees may apply if the driver is already on the way.

Service may not yet cover your area, or GPS is weak. Try moving to a nearby main road or check that location services are turned on.

Drivers may cancel due to emergencies, traffic, or vehicle issues. You can simply book another ride right away.

Try calling or messaging the driver in-app. If there is no response, cancel the ride and report the issue through Help Center.

After the trip ends, a rating screen will appear automatically. Select the number of stars and leave feedback if needed.

Check the destination entered before booking. If the drop-off was incorrect due to the trip, report it under Trip History → Report an Issue.

Match the driver’s name, photo, vehicle model, color, and plate number shown in the app before getting in.

We are sorry to hear that you've misplaced an item during your recent trip. We are here to help you recover it.

Please fill out the form below with your booking details so we can contact the driver on your behalf. In the 'Description' field, please provide:

  • Item Details: A specific description (e.g., color, brand, model, or contents).
  • Location: Where the item was last seen (e.g., "front seat," "backseat pocket," or "trunk").

Once the driver confirms the item is found, we will notify you immediately with instructions on how to claim it. Please keep in mind that our drivers follow busy schedules, but we will coordinate with them as quickly as possible to facilitate a return.

How to find your driver's name: You can view your driver's details by going to the History section of your app and selecting your most recent booking.

Claiming Lost Property: If your item has been surrendered to our office by a driver, we will notify you immediately via email or phone. We will provide you with a reference number and the necessary details (location and hours) on how to safely claim your property.

Claiming Process for Walk-ins

To ensure your lost item is returned to the correct owner, please follow these steps when visiting our office:

  • Proof of Ownership: Provide your Booking ID and a detailed description of the item and its contents.
  • Identification: Present a valid government-issued ID to the Security Office for verification.
  • Acknowledgment: Once the item is verified, you will be required to sign and date the Lost and Found Property Form to confirm receipt.

Important note: While we will do our best to search, unfortunately, we cannot guarantee that an item can be found or delivered immediately. Neither our driver-partner nor Move It is responsible for any loss or damage to any of the items left in the car after a trip ends. Please note that all food and perishable items will be disposed of after 24 hours.
